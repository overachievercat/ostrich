Ostrich

A private daily look at your money.
Every number is welcome.

Your numbers stay on this device. Ostrich has no account and no server for them. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere.

Ostrich won't tell you what's right or wrong to do with your money. It shows you where you are.

How does it feel to look at your money?

There is no right answer. This only sets your pace.

Starting gently. Amounts start hidden, and you can show them when you're ready. You can change this any time.

If it feels like too much today, that's okay.

Not a score. Ostrich doesn't grade this answer or share it. It stays on this device, and Ostrich is not therapy.

What was sent?

Nothing. This prototype has no AI yet, and its code makes no network requests. Your numbers, your feeling and your history stay on this device.

When the private AI reading arrives (on the roadmap)

Only totals like these would go to the AI, never account names, institutions or wallet addresses. The reading would run in a sealed, private computer on NEAR AI Cloud, so no one, including Ostrich, could see them. Today, none of this is sent:


    
Your feeling and your history would stay on this device unless you chose to include them. Check it yourself: the code is open source.

    
  


  
  

    
Want to talk to someone?

    
Ostrich is not therapy, and you don't have to do this alone. Free options come first, always.

    

      
A nonprofit credit counselor
Free or low-cost help with debt and a plan. Find one through the National Foundation for Credit Counseling: nfcc.org

      
Free tax help, and money you may be owed
IRS volunteer sites (VITA) prepare taxes for free if you qualify: irs.gov

      
Someone you trust
A friend or family member. Hiding a worry to protect someone can make it heavier to carry. Try: "Money has been hard lately. I don't need you to fix it. I didn't want to keep it from you."

    

    

      
Is someone controlling your money?

      
Taking your pay, checking every dollar, keeping you from work or putting debt in your name can be a kind of abuse. It is not your fault.

      
The National Domestic Violence Hotline is free and private: call 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788. Need to leave fast? Tap Hide all at the top.

    

    

      
If things feel unsafe

      
In the US, call or text 988 any time.
