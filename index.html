Your number, kept up to date for you.

    
Today you type three numbers. Later, Ostrich fills them in every morning from your accounts, read only. Your job stays the same: look once a day. When your money life changes, like a new card or a new wallet, you add it here.

    
Every morning (concept)
Your sources refresh before you wake up, and the totals are added up on your phone. You look. Ostrich never moves money and never sees your passwords.

How the private AI reading would work

    

      
On your phone
Ostrich adds your accounts up into a few totals. Account names, institutions and wallet addresses stay on your phone.

      
Into a sealed computer
The totals go to an AI running inside a trusted execution environment (TEE) on NEAR AI Cloud: Intel TDX with NVIDIA confidential GPUs. Memory is sealed, so not the cloud operator, not NEAR and not Ostrich can see them.

      
A proof from the chips
The hardware signs an attestation: proof of exactly which model and code ran, inside a genuine enclave. Anyone can check it against Intel's and NVIDIA's attestation services or a public TEE explorer, the way anyone can look up a transaction on a block explorer.

      
A reading comes back
A short, calm reading of your totals, signed by a key that never leaves the enclave, returns to your phone.

Where your money data comes from

    
All your money is real-world finance plus crypto. Crypto is what NEAR can reach plus the rest. Over time, both arrows grow.

    
All your money (w = t + k) · illustrative, not to scale

    
Real-world finance (t)Banks, cards, loans, stocks
Crypto (k)Every chain

    
Crypto (k = x + n)

    
Reached through NEAR (x)NEAR, plus other chains via Chain Signatures and NEAR Intents
The rest (n)Add by wallet address


    

      
How Ostrich would read each part

      
x, through NEAR: automatically, read only, each morning.

      
n, other crypto: by wallet address, until NEAR reaches it.

      
t, real-world finance: Plaid, statements, or by hand.

    

    

      
Both arrows grow

      
Stocks, gold, real estate and more are becoming tokens, so part of t moves into k. More chains become reachable through NEAR, so part of n moves into x.

      
Others are building tools to bring more data into NEAR. Ostrich plans to plug into them as they arrive, rather than build them.
